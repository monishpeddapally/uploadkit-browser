# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, and focus criticism on ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile.
